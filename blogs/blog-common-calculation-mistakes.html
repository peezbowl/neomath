5 Common Calculation Mistakes in Class 8 & 9 (And How to Fix Them)

From sign inversions in minus brackets to incorrect cancellations across addition signs — master the 3-second self-audit routine before handing in your exam paper.

As a mathematics teacher, I see bright students lose 5 to 10 marks in every exam not because they didn't understand the concept, but because of predictable, avoidable "silly mistakes."

When you identify the exact psychological and visual traps where errors occur, you can train your eyes to catch them instantly. Here are the five most frequent culprits:

1. The Minus Sign Outside a Bracket: -(a - b)

When distributing a negative sign into parentheses, students frequently change the first term's sign and forget to flip the second term's sign:

❌ Common Mistake:
-(3x - 5) = -3x - 5 (Incorrect!)
✅ Correct Way:
-(3x - 5) = -3x + 5 (Both terms flip!)

Teacher's Tip: Mentally rewrite -(...) as (-1) × (...) to force yourself to multiply each term by -1.

2. The "Illegal Cancellation" Trap

One of the most dangerous reflexes when simplifying fractions:

❌ Common Mistake:
Cancelling x in (x + 4) / x to get 4.
✅ Golden Rule:
You can ONLY cancel factors (terms that are multiplied), never addends!

(x + 4) / x = x/x + 4/x = 1 + 4/x. You cannot cancel across a plus or minus sign!

3. Squaring a Binomial: (a + b)² is NOT a² + b²

Forgetting the middle term 2ab is an epidemic in Class 8 and 9 algebra:

Always Write the Full Identity
(a + b)² = a² + 2ab + b²
(a - b)² = a² - 2ab + b²

Test with numbers: (3 + 2)² = 5² = 25. But 3² + 2² = 9 + 4 = 13 ≠ 25. The missing 12 is 2 × 3 × 2!

4. Forgetting Units in Mensuration

Finding area in cm² vs volume in cm³ vs perimeter in cm. When questions give radius in meters and height in centimeters, convert them to the same unit first before applying any formula!

5. The 3-Second Exam Self-Audit Routine

Before moving to the next problem in your exam, spend 3 seconds asking yourself:

  • Did I copy the numbers correctly from the question paper?
  • Does the sign make sense (e.g. lengths and ages can never be negative)?
  • Did I include the appropriate units (cm², kg, rupees)?

CLASS 8 MATHEMATICS

Build strong foundations. Understand the "why", not just the "how".

CHAPTERS & SYLLABUS

01

Rational Numbers

Properties, number line representation, and operations

Understanding numbers that can be expressed as p/q where q ≠ 0, their closure, commutativity, associativity, and density between any two rational numbers.

Key Concepts: Closure, Commutative, Associative and Distributive properties • Role of Zero (additive identity) and One (multiplicative identity) • Additive inverse & Multiplicative inverse (Reciprocal) • Finding infinite rational numbers between any two rational numbers
02

Linear Equations in One Variable

Transposition, algebraic modeling, and problem solving

Equations of the form ax + b = c. Learn techniques to isolate variables, balance both sides, and translate word problems into crisp equations.

Key Concepts: Concept of linear expression (degree 1) • Solving equations having linear expressions on one side and numbers on the other • Solving equations having variables on both sides • Word problems: Ages, perimeters, consecutive numbers, and coin problems
03

Understanding Quadrilaterals

Polygons, angles sum property, and special quadrilaterals

Dive deep into regular and irregular polygons, convex and concave shapes, exterior angle sums, parallelograms, rhombuses, rectangles, and kites.

Key Concepts: Classification of polygons (convex, concave, regular) • Angle sum property of convex n-sided polygons: (n - 2) * 180° • Sum of exterior angles of any polygon = 360° • Properties of Parallelograms, Rhombus, Rectangle, Square, and Kite
04

Data Handling

Organizing data, frequency tables, histograms, and pie charts

Learn how to interpret frequency distributions, draw clear pie charts, compute central angles, and understand basic probability of events.

Key Concepts: Grouped frequency distribution and Class intervals • Histograms vs Bar Graphs (continuous ranges vs discrete bars) • Pie charts (circle graphs) & Central angle formula • Chance and Probability: Outcomes and equally likely events
05

Squares and Square Roots

Patterns, Pythagorean triplets, prime factorisation, and long division

Discover intriguing patterns in square numbers, unit digit rules, finding square roots by subtraction, prime factorisation, and the division method.

Key Concepts: Properties of square numbers (unit digits: 0, 1, 4, 5, 6, 9) • Triangular numbers, consecutive odd sums, and Pythagorean triplets • Square roots via repeated odd subtraction and prime factorisation • Long division method for large numbers and decimal square roots
06

Cubes and Cube Roots

Hardy-Ramanujan numbers, prime factorisation, and estimation

Explore numbers like 1729, units digits of cubes, finding cube roots using prime factorisation and the fast estimation method for perfect cubes.

Key Concepts: Units digit behavior of cube numbers • Adding consecutive odd numbers to form cubes • Cube roots by prime factorisation method • Fast estimation technique for cube roots of up to 6-digit numbers
07

Comparing Quantities

Ratios, percentages, profit & loss, discount, GST, and compound interest

Practical financial mathematics: converting ratios to percentages, markup and discount, GST calculation, and compounding interest annually vs half-yearly.

Key Concepts: Increase / Decrease percentage calculations • Marked price, selling price, and discount percentage • Goods and Services Tax (GST) applications • Compound Interest formula: Annual vs Semi-annual compounding
08

Algebraic Expressions & Identities

Monomials, polynomials, standard identities, and applications

Master algebraic multiplication, like and unlike terms, and the 4 fundamental identities that form the backbone of high school mathematics.

Key Concepts: Terms, coefficients, monomials, binomials, and polynomials • Multiplication of polynomials (binomial with binomial, trinomials) • Standard Identity I: (a + b)² = a² + 2ab + b² • Standard Identity II: (a - b)² = a² - 2ab + b² • Standard Identity III: (a + b)(a - b) = a² - b² • Identity IV: (x + a)(x + b) = x² + (a + b)x + ab

CLASS 9 MATHEMATICS

Build strong foundations. Understand the "why", not just the "how".

CHAPTERS & SYLLABUS

01

Number Systems

Irrational numbers, real numbers on number line, and rationalisation

Dive into irrational numbers (like √2, √3, π), decimal expansions (terminating vs non-terminating recurring), representing roots on number line via spiral, and rationalising denominators.

Key Concepts: Real numbers = Rational numbers + Irrational numbers • Decimal expansions: Terminating, non-terminating repeating, non-terminating non-repeating • Locating √x on the number line using geometric Pythagoras spirals • Operations on real numbers and laws of exponents for real exponents • Rationalisation of denominators with binomial quadratic surds
02

Polynomials

Zeroes, Remainder Theorem, Factor Theorem, and higher identities

Explore degrees of polynomials, coefficients, Remainder theorem, Factor theorem, and algebraic identities like (a+b+c)², (a+b)³, and a³+b³+c³ - 3abc.

Key Concepts: Degree of polynomials and classification (linear, quadratic, cubic) • Zeroes/roots of a polynomial: p(k) = 0 • Remainder Theorem and Factor Theorem • Splitting the middle term for quadratic polynomials • Factorising cubic polynomials using trial zero and synthetic division
03

Coordinate Geometry

Cartesian plane, quadrants, abscissa, ordinate, and plotting points

Understand René Descartes invention connecting algebra and geometry through Cartesian coordinates, axes, four quadrants, and plotting ordered pairs.

Key Concepts: Origin (0,0), X-axis (abscissa) and Y-axis (ordinate) • Four quadrants and signs: (+,+), (-,+), (-,-), (+,-) • Coordinates of points on the axes: (x, 0) and (0, y) • Plotting geometric shapes on the Cartesian coordinate system
04

Linear Equations in Two Variables

Standard form ax + by + c = 0, graph plotting, and infinite solutions

Examine linear equations in two variables. Learn why every linear equation in two variables represents a straight line with infinitely many solutions.

Key Concepts: Standard form ax + by + c = 0 (a, b not both zero) • Geometric representation: Every solution (x, y) is a point on the line • Finding 4 solutions to graph a line with precision • Lines parallel to axes: x = a and y = b
05

Lines and Angles

Parallel lines, transversals, linear pairs, and angle theorems

Learn key geometric axioms and theorems: vertically opposite angles, linear pair axiom, corresponding angles, alternate interior angles, and triangle angle sum theorem.

Key Concepts: Axioms: Linear pair axiom and Adjacent angles • Vertically opposite angles theorem • Transversal intersecting parallel lines (corresponding, alternate interior, consecutive interior angles) • Angle sum property of triangle (180°) and exterior angle theorem
06

Triangles & Congruence

SAS, ASA, AAS, SSS, and RHS criteria, and triangle inequalities

Rigorous geometric proofs of congruence criteria, isosceles triangle theorems, CPCTC (Corresponding Parts of Congruent Triangles are Congruent).

Key Concepts: Concept of Congruence: Exact same shape and size • Congruence Criteria: SAS, ASA, AAS, SSS, RHS • Angles opposite to equal sides of an isosceles triangle are equal • CPCTC application in multi-step geometric proofs
07

Circles

Chords, subtended angles, cyclic quadrilaterals, and circle theorems

Discover the elegance of circle theorems: equal chords subtend equal angles at centre, angle at centre is double the angle at circumference, and opposite angles of cyclic quadrilateral sum to 180°.

Key Concepts: Perpendicular from centre to chord bisects the chord • Equal chords are equidistant from centre • Angle subtended by arc at centre is twice angle at circumference • Angles in the same segment of a circle are equal • Opposite angles of a cyclic quadrilateral are supplementary (sum = 180°)
08

Heron's Formula & Surface Areas

Triangles without height, spheres, cones, and cylinders

Calculate triangle areas using Heron's formula s = (a+b+c)/2, and find total and curved surface areas and volumes of cones, cylinders, and spheres.

Key Concepts: Heron's formula for scalene triangles with all three sides known • Curved surface area and volume of right circular cones (l² = r² + h²) • Surface area and volume of spheres (4πr²) and hemispheres (3πr²)
